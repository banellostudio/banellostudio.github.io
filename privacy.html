ExitWise privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026 (play statistics sent to Google Analytics; first version 20 October 2026)

ExitWise ("the game") is made by Banello Studio, run by Paweł Laban, Moniuszki 7/7B, 26-610 Radom, Poland, NIP 9482645590 ("we"). Contact for anything about this policy or your data: banellostudio@proton.me.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone.
  • Play statistics (which levels you solve, your moves, hints and skips) are sent to Google Analytics only if you say yes when the game asks, and you can switch them off in Settings at any time. We use them to design better daily heists and new levels.
  • Ads are optional: you only see one when you tap "Watch an ad" for hints. Google AdMob shows them, and in the EU/UK Google asks for your consent first.
  • Purchases (the full version) go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Data saved on your phone

Your progress (levels solved, stars, settings, saved hints, the daily streak) is stored in the game's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Play statistics (only with your consent)

When you start the game for the first time, it asks: "Help improve ExitWise?". Only if you choose Share statistics do we collect:

  • game events: a level started, solved, left or skipped; the number of moves, seconds, undos, restarts, hints used, times caught; ads watched for hints; the full-version card shown or bought; daily heist solved; a hat bought; the Share button used;
  • a random install ID that the game creates the first time it sends statistics (not your device's ID or advertising ID), and a random number for each time the game starts;
  • the game version, whether the full version is owned, the operating system (Android) and the country from your phone's language setting (for example "PL").

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location or advertising ID for statistics. Purpose: to find levels that are too hard or too easy and to design new ones (legal basis: your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

The statistics are sent to Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, which processes them for us. They go through Google's servers in the EU and are kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically. Google receives your IP address as part of the internet connection; Google Analytics does not store it. Google may transfer data to the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in which Google takes part (see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services). If you play offline, events wait on your phone (at most 3 days) and are sent later.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Statistics: off. Nothing is collected or sent after that, and the install ID and any statistics still waiting on your phone are deleted. Statistics already sent cannot be linked to you by name; they are deleted after 14 months.

3. Ads (only when you choose to watch one)

The game shows a video ad only when you tap Watch an ad to get saved hints. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and interactions with the ad, and may show personalised ads if you allow it. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is loaded; you can change it later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Legal basis: your consent (personalised ads) or Google's legitimate interest as described by Google. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

The full version is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase exists (to unlock the game on your devices). See Google Play's terms.

5. Children

ExitWise is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has shared data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to banellostudio@proton.me. Step by step: how to delete your ExitWise data. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. Important changes are also announced in the game.

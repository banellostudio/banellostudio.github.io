ExitWise pre-registration reward

ExitWise: Heist Puzzle, by Banello Studio · 30 October 2026

The reward

The Early agent hat: a hat for your spy, shown in the game's Wardrobe. It is free and needs no purchase. It only changes how your spy looks: it doesn't open rooms or make the game easier.

Who gets it

Anyone who pre-registers for ExitWise on Google Play before the game launches, in a country where the store page shows the reward. Pre-registration ends when the game launches.

Players in our closed test can't pre-register, so they get the hat inside the game.

How you get it

  1. Tap Pre-register on the ExitWise page on Google Play.
  2. After launch, install the game with the same Google account.
  3. Open the game while connected to the internet. The hat appears in your Wardrobe.

Good to know

  • One hat per Google account.
  • It stays with that Google account, also if you reinstall the game or move to a new phone.
  • It has no cash value and can't be exchanged, sold or moved to another account.

Questions

If the hat doesn't appear, write to banellostudio@proton.me. How the game handles your data: ExitWise privacy policy.

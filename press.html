In one sentence

ExitWise is a turn-based heist puzzle where every rule of the building is a chip you can unplug.

Description

You're a spy, and every building has rules. Walls stop you, guards chase you, lasers watch the halls. But those rules are chips plugged into consoles, and you can push them around. Unplug STOP from the WALL console and you walk through walls. Push CRATE onto the GUARD console and every guard turns into a crate. Guards only take a step when you do, so nothing moves until you move.

Each building adds a new trick: wet floors and lasers at the Docks, conveyor belts at the Train Station, vents and security cameras at the Museum, and a secret fifth building for those who crack every room.

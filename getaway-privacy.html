ExitWise Getaway privacy policy

Effective date: (the day this page goes online)

ExitWise Getaway ("the game") is made by Banello Studio, run by Paweł Laban, Moniuszki 7/7B, 26-610 Radom, Poland, NIP 9482645590 ("we"). Contact for anything about this policy or your data: banellostudio@proton.me.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone. The game sends us no statistics.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad between some levels, and video ads you choose to watch for a reward. In the EU/UK Google asks for your consent first.
  • Purchases (coin packs, the starter pack, Remove ads) go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Data saved on your phone

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, coins, upgrades, settings, and the record of purchases already granted) is stored in the game's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may also keep an encrypted copy in your own Google account, so the game can restore your progress if you reinstall it or move to a new phone; we never receive or see that copy. Uninstalling the game deletes it from your phone; the backup copy is managed in your Google account.

2. Ads

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then after a cleared level (never in the first levels, never during a wave, and never after you buy anything in the game), and video ads only when you tap a "watch ad" button for a reward (a revive, double coins, a bigger chest, coins). Ads in the game are limited to content rated for teens (T) at most. To show and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and interactions with the ad, and may show personalised ads if you allow it. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are loaded; you can change it later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Legal basis: your consent (personalised ads) or Google's legitimate interest as described by Google. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

Coin packs, the starter pack and Remove ads are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the game only receives confirmation that a purchase exists (a purchase token), so it can deliver it once and restore Remove ads after a reinstall (Settings → Restore purchases). See Google Play's terms.

4. Children

ExitWise Getaway is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has shared data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to banellostudio@proton.me. We hold no data about you on our side: your progress lives on your phone (deleting the game deletes it), and ad data is held by Google (see the link above, and your Google account's ad settings). You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. Important changes are also announced in the game.

Delete your ExitWise Getaway data

ExitWise Getaway: Spy Rush, by Banello Studio · 14 November 2026

ExitWise Getaway has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or contacts, and the game sends us no statistics. Here is what is kept, and how to delete it.

Your progress (on your phone)

Levels, stars, coins, upgrades and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them: in the game open Settings → Reset progress (tap twice to confirm), or uninstall the game. If Android backup is on, Android may keep an encrypted copy in your own Google account; you can delete it in your phone's Settings (Google → Backup) or in Google One. We never receive or see that copy.

Ad data (held by Google)

The ads are shown by Google AdMob. Google may process your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. To limit or delete it: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads), change your choices in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where the law asks for it), and see Google's My Ad Center and how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases (held by Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play; the game only keeps a record that a purchase was delivered, on your phone (Reset progress keeps it, so Remove Ads stays yours; uninstalling the game deletes it). Your order history is in your Google Play account.

Ask us

We hold no data about you on our side. If you have a question or want us to confirm, write to banellostudio@proton.me; we answer within 30 days. More in the privacy policy.
